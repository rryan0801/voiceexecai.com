## Getting Started

### What is VoiceExec AI?

VoiceExec AI is your personal sales assistant that runs on voice commands. Just speak, and the AI handles:
- Sending follow-up emails
- Scheduling meetings
- Creating tasks
- Logging CRM notes
- Generating proposals

All in seconds while you focus on closing deals.

### Login & Setup

**Step 1: Access Your Dashboard**

Go to: `https://voicerep.app/`

Sign in with your company email and password.

**Step 2: Connect Your Calendar (Optional)**

Click "Outlook" button on Prospect Management page to sync your calendar for meeting scheduling. This allows the AI to find your availability automatically.

**Step 3: Familiarize Yourself**

Tour the dashboard (left sidebar navigation):
- **Dashboard** - Overview of your activity
- **Deals** - See which prospects are closest to closing
- **Conversations** - View your successful email patterns
- **Meeting Prep** - Get AI-coached before calls
- **Playbooks** - Follow proven sequences your manager created
- **Analytics** - Track your impact
- **Commands** - See history of all voice commands
- **Prospects** - Manage your prospect database

## Using the Voice Widget

### Where is the Widget?

The widget is a **floating microphone button** in the bottom-right corner of any website your company has integrated it with (usually your internal portal or CRM).

### How to Record a Command

**Step 1: Click the Microphone 🎤**

The button will turn red to indicate recording is active.

**Step 2: Speak Your Command**

Keep it natural and direct. Examples:

- "Send a follow-up email to John at Acme saying we discussed the proposal"
- "Schedule a demo call with Sarah for Friday at 2 PM"
- "Create a task to follow up with the prospect if they don't reply in 3 days"
- "Log to HubSpot that we talked about pricing objections today"

**Step 3: Release the Button**

The widget will show you:
1. **Transcription** - What it heard
2. **AI Reasoning** - What action it's about to take
3. **Result** - Confirmation of what was done

### Providing Context

Before recording, you can optionally add:
- Prospect Name
- Company Name

Click the **"Add Context"** dropdown to fill these in. This helps the AI write more personalized emails.

### What Commands Work?

| Intent | Example |
|--------|---------|
| **Send Email** | "Email John saying thanks for the call" |
| **Schedule Call** | "Schedule a meeting with Sarah on Friday at 3 PM" |
| **Create Task** | "Remind me to follow up if they don't reply" |
| **Log CRM** | "Log this call to HubSpot with objection noted" |
| **Generate Document** | "Create a proposal for the enterprise plan" |

**Pro Tip:** The more specific you are, the better the result. Instead of "Send email," try "Send follow-up email to prospect saying we're ready to move forward."

### If Something Goes Wrong

**Error Messages:**

| Message | What to Do |
|---------|-----------|
| "Microphone access denied" | Grant browser permission (Settings → Microphone) |
| "Empty transcription" | Speak louder and longer (min 3 seconds) |
| "Action failed" | Check internet connection, try again |

## Deal Intelligence

### What is Deal Intelligence?

The AI scores all your prospects based on:
- **Interaction frequency** - How many times you've touched this deal
- **Recent activity** - Calls/emails in the last 7 days
- **AutoPilot progress** - How far through your follow-up sequence
- **Engagement signals** - Did they reply? Show interest?

**Result:** A win probability (0-100%) so you know where to focus.

### How to Use It

**Path:** Sidebar → "Deals"

**What You See:**
- **Ranked List** - Deals sorted by win probability
- **Probability Score** - 0-100%, color-coded:
  - 🟢 75%+ = High probability (close soon)
  - 🔵 50-74% = Medium (keep nurturing)
  - 🟡 25-49% = Low (needs attention)
  - ⚫ <25% = Very low (risky)

**Recommended Actions:**
- 75%+ → "Schedule Call" (push for commitment)
- 50-74% → "Send Proposal" (move deal forward)
- <50% → "Nurture" (stay top of mind) or "Check In" (regain interest)

### Expanding a Deal

Click any deal card to see:
- Score breakdown (interaction + recency + AutoPilot + engagement scores)
- Recent interactions
- Last contact date
- AutoPilot sequence progress

## Conversation Analytics

### What is Conversation Analytics?

The AI analyzes your successful emails to find patterns:
- **Closing phrases** - Words/phrases that lead to closes
- **Winning keywords** - Terms you use that convert
- **Tone profile** - Your communication style
- **Opening approaches** - How you typically start conversations

### How to Use It

**Path:** Sidebar → "Conversations"

**Step 1: Select a Rep**

Click on a teammate to see their patterns. (If viewing yourself, the page auto-selects you.)

**Step 2: Click "Analyze"**

The system scans your past emails and extracts patterns.

**Step 3: Review Results**

**Closing Phrases:**
- Shows exact phrases you used
- Success rate (% of emails with this phrase that closed)
- Frequency (how often you use it)
- Coaching tip

**Example:**
```
"Let's move forward together"
✓ 67% win rate | Used 12x
💡 Tip: This collaborative language resonates with risk-averse buyers
```

**Winning Keywords:**
- Single words or short phrases correlated with closes
- Examples: "ROI," "proven track record," "risk-free"

**Your Tone Profile:**
- Formality level (formal, semi-professional, casual)
- Urgency (high, moderate, low)
- Personal touch (how often you reference specific details)

### How to Apply These Insights

**In the Widget:**
When you draft an email via voice, the system will rewrite it to match your tone and incorporate your winning keywords.

**In Training:**
Share your best patterns with teammates. If "Let's move forward together" works for you, they can try it too.

## Meeting Copilot

### What is Meeting Copilot?

Before every important call, get AI-generated intel:
- **Talking points** - Key things to mention
- **Likely objections** - What they might push back on
- **Closing approach** - How to ask for the deal
- **Win indicators** - Signs they're ready to close
- **Conversation starter** - How to open the call

### How to Use It

**Path:** Sidebar → "Meeting Prep"

**Step 1: Search for Your Prospect**

Type their name or company in the search box.

**Step 2: Click "Get Call Intel"**

The AI analyzes:
- Your past interactions with them
- Similar companies you've closed
- Your playbooks for their deal stage

**Step 3: Review the Intel**

**Key Talking Points:**
Read these before the call. Reference specific wins if relevant.

**Example:**
```
✓ Their biggest pain is slow order fulfillment
✓ Company has 50+ field reps (good ICP fit)
✓ They mentioned budget approval timeline in last email
```

**Likely Objections:**
Prepare your rebuttal. Click → "Objection Handler" if they actually bring up an objection during the call.

**Closing Approach:**
Suggested way to ask for the business.

**Win Indicators:**
Listen for these during the call—if you hear them, they're ready to close.

**Best Practices:**
- Review intel 10 minutes before the call
- Take notes during call on which talking points resonated
- Share feedback if intel was inaccurate (helps AI improve)

## Email Tone Matching

### What is Email Tone Matching?

The AI rewrites your draft emails to match your personal communication style, so all your emails sound like you.

**Benefits:**
- Consistency (prospects recognize your voice)
- Efficiency (AI handles grammar/polish)
- Authenticity (still your ideas, just better phrased)

### How to Use It

**In the Widget:**

When you command "Send email to [prospect]," you can optionally say the body:

```
"Send email to John saying: hey man I wanted to circle back on the 
proposal we discussed. let me know if you have questions."
```

**The AI will:**
1. Analyze your last 3 emails for tone/style
2. Rewrite for clarity, grammar, professionalism
3. Show you the rewritten version before sending

**Example:**

**Your Draft:**
```
hey dude, we should talk about moving forward with this. lmk if ur interested
```

**AI Rewrite (matching your casual-but-professional tone):**
```
Hi John, wanted to loop back on that proposal. Let me know if you're ready to move forward—happy to discuss any questions you have. Best, [Your name]
```

**Step: Accept or Edit**

You'll see the rewritten email and can:
- Accept and send
- Make edits
- Ask AI to rewrite again

## Team Playbooks

### What are Playbooks?

Playbooks are step-by-step sequences created by your sales manager for closing deals at each stage:

- **Prospecting** - How to open conversations
- **Qualification** - How to uncover their needs
- **Proposal** - How to present your solution
- **Negotiation** - How to handle pricing objections
- **Closing** - How to ask for the deal

### How to Use Them

**Path:** Sidebar → "Playbooks"

**Step 1: Find the Right Playbook**

Filter by deal stage or search by industry. Example:
- "Q2 Enterprise Close" (negotiation stage, enterprise deals)
- "Quick SMB Demo" (proposal stage, small business)

**Step 2: Click "Use This Playbook"**

The playbook will populate your follow-up sequence. It includes:
- Multi-step email templates
- Timing (e.g., "Send email, wait 3 days, send video call invite")
- Conditions (e.g., "If they don't reply, send second touch")

**Step 3: AutoPilot Runs It**

If enabled, AutoPilot will automatically execute the steps. If not, you'll get reminders to manually send emails.

**Step 4: Track Results**

The playbook tracks if deals close. Over time, your manager refines the playbooks based on success rates.

## Objection Handler

### What is Objection Handler?

During a call, if the prospect raises an objection, use this tool to get real-time responses.

**Available 24/7, helps with:**
- Price objections ("Your solution is too expensive")
- Timeline concerns ("We need to wait until Q3")
- Competitor comparisons ("Why should we switch from [competitor]?")
- Implementation fears ("This seems complex to set up")

### How to Use It

**During a Call:**

1. Pause briefly
2. Open VoiceExec dashboard on your laptop/tablet
3. Click "Conversations" → "Objection Handler"
4. Enter the objection: "They said pricing is too high"

**The AI Returns:**

```json
{
  "immediate_response": "I understand price is a factor. Let me show 
                        you the ROI—most clients recoup their investment 
                        in 4 months.",
  "follow_up_actions": [
    "Share ROI calculator",
    "Offer 30-day free trial",
    "Introduce them to a successful customer"
  ],
  "reframe_approach": "Position as cost savings, not cost"
}
```

**Apply:** Use the immediate response while on the call.

## Tips & Best Practices

### Voice Command Tips

**1. Be Specific**
- ❌ "Send an email"
- ✅ "Send a follow-up email to Jane saying we're ready to move forward and asking if she can meet Thursday"

**2. Provide Context**
- Always include prospect name
- Mention company if relevant
- Hint at tone (casual, formal, urgent)

**3. Use Natural Language**
- Don't try to be robotic
- The AI understands conversational speech
- Pauses are okay; the system waits

**4. Test First**
- Record a test command on a non-critical prospect
- See how the AI interprets it
- Adjust your phrasing if needed

### Email Best Practices

**1. Review Before Sending**
- Always read the AI-rewritten email
- Make sure tone matches your brand
- Check for factual accuracy

**2. A/B Test**
- Note which phrases/approaches get replies
- The AI learns from your sent emails
- Your patterns improve over time

**3. Timing Matters**
- Early morning (7-9am) = high open rate
- Late afternoon follow-ups = good for scheduling
- Friday emails = lower engagement (try Monday instead)

### Call Preparation

**1. Use Meeting Copilot 10 min Before**
- Read the talking points
- Review objections they're likely to raise
- Prep your closes

**2. Take Notes During the Call**
- AI will use these in future intel
- More data = better recommendations

**3. Log the Outcome**
- Update deal status after the call
- Mark objections encountered
- This feedback trains the system

### AutoPilot Sequences

**1. Let It Run**
- Set up a playbook
- Let AutoPilot handle the timing
- Your job: respond when they reply

**2. Monitor Progress**
- Check AutoPilot page weekly
- Pause if they go silent (signal lost interest)
- Adjust if reply detected (move to next stage)

**3. Personalize When Needed**
- AutoPilot uses templates
- Add personal touches in email body
- Prospects notice and respond better

### Improving Your Patterns

**1. Weekly Review**
- Check Conversation Analytics weekly
- See what's working
- Adopt teammates' successful phrases

**2. Document Wins**
- When you close a deal, note what worked
- Was it a specific phrase? Objection handling? Timing?
- Manager can add these to playbooks

**3. Ask for Feedback**
- Manager can review your patterns
- Get coaching on objection handling
- Learn from top performers' techniques

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use VoiceExec on my phone?**

A: Yes! Click "Mobile" in the sidebar or go directly to the mobile URL. You can record voice commands anywhere.

**Q: What if the transcription is wrong?**

A: The AI shows you the transcription before acting. If it's wrong, just cancel and try again with clearer speech.

**Q: Does AutoPilot respect my calendar?**

A: Yes, if you connected Outlook. AutoPilot checks your availability before scheduling meetings.

**Q: Can I undo a command?**

A: If executed seconds ago, you can see the result and manually undo (e.g., delete email in Outlook). After that, contact your manager to remove from CRM.

**Q: Do you track email open rates?**

A: Not yet. Recommended: Use Outlook read receipts or CRM tracking for this.

**Q: Can I customize my playbooks?**

A: Only your manager can edit playbooks. Suggest improvements to them.

**Q: Is my data secure?**

A: Yes. All data encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. OAuth scopes limit what AI can access.
